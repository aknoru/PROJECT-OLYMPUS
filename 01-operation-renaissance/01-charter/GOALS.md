# The 31 Goals of Project Olympus

**Owner:** Rounak Prajapati
**Timeline:** 184 Days (August 3, 2026 – January 31, 2027)
**Mission:** Complete Personal Renaissance

> You cannot achieve all 31 in one day. You cannot achieve all 31 in one week. You can achieve them one day at a time. Your daily checklist is your compass. Your weekly review is your course correction. Your monthly milestones are your progress markers.

## Category 1: Career & Financial (Goals 1–6)

### Goal 1: Secure a Paid Internship
- **Target:** ₹50,000–55,000/month minimum stipend
- **Domains:** FPGA, RTL Design, ASIC, VLSI, Embedded Systems, Edge AI, Semiconductor, Hardware-Software Co-design

### Goal 2: Placement Goal
- **Dream:** ₹45–50 LPA+ package
- **Acceptable:** ₹20–25 LPA minimum
- **Ultimate:** Semiconductor company, Global hardware company, High-end systems engineering

### Goal 3: Remote Work
- **Target Countries:** Taiwan, Korea, Singapore
- **Purpose:** Earn in foreign currency, Build savings, Escape low-paying local opportunities

### Goal 4: Financial Goals
- **Initial Target:** Earn ₹6,00,000 within six months
- **Long-term:** ₹2,00,000/month through AI Agency, Freelancing, Automation, Consulting, Software, Electronics
- **Eventually:** Passive income

### Goal 5: AI Automation Agency
- Build a profitable AI Automation Agency
- **Services:** GenAI, AI Agents, Workflow Automation, Business Automation, LLM Systems
- **Goal:** Real clients, Real revenue

### Goal 6: Entrepreneurship
- **Create:** AI Agency, Freelancing, Products, Services, Consulting, Automation Systems
