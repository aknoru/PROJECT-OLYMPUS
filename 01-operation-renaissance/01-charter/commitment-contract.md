# Commitment Contract

## Purpose

Record an informed, printable agreement to operate the campaign.

## Scope

This agreement confirms process responsibilities. It does not guarantee an
external result and may be amended or ended through documented review.

## Theory

Commitment is credible when duties, limits, review points, and exit conditions
are explicit.

## Framework

### Mission

I accept the mission stated in [Mission](mission.md) for a configured 184-day
cycle.

### Responsibilities

I will plan from observed capacity, produce honest evidence, complete scheduled
reviews, protect safety and privacy, disclose assistance, and record material
decisions.

### Non-negotiables

I will not falsify evidence, misuse protected material, conceal known safety
risks, sacrifice essential recovery to “catch up,” or treat selection outcomes as
guaranteed.

### Review schedule

I will complete the review cadence authorized by later implementation sprints and
will review this agreement whenever a material constraint changes.

## Implementation

Print or copy the signature block. Complete it only after quickstart, baseline,
constraints, and exit criteria have been reviewed.

## Checklist

- [ ] I read the charter.
- [ ] I completed preflight and baseline.
- [ ] I recorded constraints.
- [ ] I understand amendment and exit rights.

## Decision Framework

Sign if the campaign is feasible and acceptable. Sign conditionally only with a
documented repair. Decline or defer when blocking constraints remain.

## Common Failure Modes

- Signing ceremonially without reviewing capacity.
- Treating the contract as irreversible.
- Leaving non-negotiables undefined.

## Recovery Strategy

Pause affected commitments, document the change, and re-sign an amended contract
only after review.

## Examples

A new medical limitation can justify immediate load reduction without waiting for
a scheduled gate.
