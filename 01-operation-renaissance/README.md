# Operation Renaissance

## Purpose

Provide canonical navigation for the 184-day engineering execution campaign.

## Scope

Sprints 03A through 03K implement the foundation, roadmap, execution, Study,
GATE, Placement, Project, Research, Technical Communication, Health, and
Resilience, Finance, Review, Recovery, Dashboard, KPI, and Appendix systems.

## Theory

The campaign follows the architecture’s separation of constitution, knowledge,
operations, evidence, and derived views. Foundation artifacts establish
requirements and constraints before downstream systems are implemented.

## Implementation

Complete quickstart and charter, configure execution, use the Study and GATE
systems, apply Placement and Project controls, and use Research and Communication
systems where relevant. Do not operate later unimplemented sections as approved.

## Checklist

- [ ] Quickstart launch gate completed.
- [ ] Charter reviewed.
- [ ] Commitment decision recorded.

## Decision Framework

Proceed beyond foundation only under a later implementation prompt and after
foundation acceptance criteria pass.

## Common Failure Modes

- Treating unimplemented sections as approved guidance.
- Skipping the baseline before committing.
- Adding unrelated systems during a bounded sprint.

## Recovery Strategy

Return to the last approved foundation artifact, identify the missing evidence,
and stop scope expansion.

## Examples

Reading `02-roadmap/README.md` does not authorize a roadmap; it remains a scaffold
until its designated sprint.
