## No Zero Days Protocol

### Definition of a Zero Day
- 0 minutes of focused work
- 0 learning progress
- 0 career progress

### Minimum Viable Day (When Everything Fails)
If you cannot do the full schedule, do this:

| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 07:00 | Wake, cold water on face, Mirror Protocol |
| 07:15 | 15 minutes of physical movement |
| 07:45 | Breakfast |
| 08:00 | 25 minutes on GATE topic |
| 13:00 | 15 minutes of DSA |
| 20:30 | 10 minutes of journaling |
| 22:00 | Sleep |

**This is the absolute minimum. No excuses. No exceptions.**

### The If-Then Protocol

| If... | Then... |
|---|---|
| I feel overwhelmed | Stop. Breathe. Do 25 minutes on one task. |
| I feel emotional | Pause. Name the emotion. Choose the right action. |
| I feel like quitting | Do 25 minutes. Then I can quit. But 25 minutes first. |
| I miss a workout | Do 15 minutes of any movement. |
| I don't hit calorie target | Track what I ate. Do better tomorrow. |
| I fail a test | Learn from the failure. Try again. |
