## Lab Standards

Every lab must include:

1. **Objectives** — what the learner will demonstrate
2. **Background** — prerequisite theory (with links)
3. **Equipment/Software** — exact tools and versions
4. **Procedure** — numbered steps, reproducible
5. **Expected Results** — measurements, outputs, waveforms
6. **Verification** — acceptance criteria (pass/fail)
7. **Observations** — data recording template
8. **Analysis Questions** — 3–5 deeper questions
9. **Troubleshooting** — common failures and fixes
10. **Extensions** — optional harder challenges

See [`templates/content/lab.md`](templates/content/lab.md) for the standard template.
