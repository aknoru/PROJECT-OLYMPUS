# 10 Entrepreneurship and Deep Tech

> **Status:** Active  
> **Domain Classification:** Deep Tech Venture Creation, IP Commercialisation & Product Engineering  
> **Target Audience:** Engineering Founders, R&D Commercialization Leads, Startup Engineers  

---

## Domain Overview

`10 Entrepreneurship and Deep Tech` guides the translation of novel hardware, silicon, and software research into commercial deep tech ventures. It spans customer discovery, technological feasibility validation, patent IP protection, regulatory safety, business model design, and venture experiments.
