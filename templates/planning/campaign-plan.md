| Phase | Days | Focus Area | Milestone Gate / Deliverable |

| **Phase 2** | Days 15–42 | Foundations & First Artifact | Core Simulation Passing, 30+ DSA Problems |
| **Phase 3** | Days 43–92 | Flagship Build & Market Test | Flagship MVP Verified, First Market Outreach |
| **Phase 4** | Days 93–142 | Package, Interview & Iterate | Flagship Released, Mock Interviews, 100+ Applications |
| **Phase 5** | Days 143–184 | Conversion & Handoff | Live Interviews, Paid Trials, Final Evidence Pack |

## 6. Traffic-Light Control & Fallback Rules

- **Green:** Health stable, sleep ≥7.5h, 80% weekly goals completed.
- **Yellow:** Missed target for 1 week. Cut extras, reduce capacity by 20%, repair bottleneck.
- **Red:** Health/sleep failure or academic risk. Revert to Minimum Viable Day (25m core + 10m review).
