## ORDER 2: THE BURNOUT PROTOCOL

### "When the engine overheats, you don't destroy it—you cool it down."

**SIGNS OF BURNOUT (Watch for these):**

| Signal | What It Looks Like |
| --- | --- |
| **Cognitive** | Can't focus for 3+ days. Reading the same paragraph repeatedly. |
| **Emotional** | Constant frustration, hopelessness, or numbness. Emotional flashbacks increasing. |
| **Physical** | Sleep <6 hours for 2 nights. Extreme fatigue. Headaches. Loss of appetite. |
| **Motivation** | Zero desire to start. Procrastination for >2 days. |

**BURNOUT PROTOCOL (If you see any signal):**

| Stage | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Stop** | Immediately stop all work. Put down books. Close laptop. |
| **2. Rest** | Sleep 10 hours. Eat a full meal. Drink 2L water. |
| **3. Reset** | Next day: Only do the Minimum Viable Day (see below) for 3 days. |
| **4. Recover** | Reduce study to 4 hours/day for 1 week. |
| **5. Return** | After 1 week, gradually add back blocks (1 extra hour per day). |
| **6. Prevent** | After recovery, build in 1 rest day/week (Sunday afternoon/evening). |

**Emergency Burnout Protocol (If you feel like quitting):**

1. Write down: *"I am burned out. This is not failure. This is my body telling me to rest."*
2. **Take 24 hours off.** No screens. No work. Walk outside. Talk to someone.
3. **Resume with 2 hours only.** Just DSA + therapy. Nothing else.
4. **Build back slowly.**

### "Protect your attention like a fortress."

**PHONE:**

- **Block 1 (08:00-12:00):** Phone in another room. On Silent.
- **Block 2 (12:00-16:00):** Phone in another room. On Silent.
- **Block 3 (16:00-19:30):** Phone in another room. On Silent.
- **Check phone only at:** 14:00 (5 min) and 19:30 (10 min).
- **Apps to Uninstall:** Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat, Twitter/X, Reddit, YouTube (use browser only for educational content).
- **Chrome/Firefox Extensions:** Block distracting sites (Cold Turkey, StayFocusd).

**FAMILY:**

- **Call family:** Only at 19:30 (10-15 min).
- **If they stress you:** Say: *"I love you, but I need to focus on my studies right now. I'll call you tomorrow."* Hang up if needed.

**FRIENDS:**

- **Tell them:** *"I'm unavailable until January 31, 2027. I'll reach out after. Please support me."*
- **No social outings.** No late-night chats.

**PROCRASTINATION:**

- **Use the 5-Minute Rule:** *"I'll do this for 5 minutes. Then I can stop."* (You won't stop—you'll continue.)
- **If you catch yourself scrolling:** Stand up. Splash cold water on face. Return to work.
- **Write down what distracts you:** Then throw the paper away. It's mental clutter.

**EMERGENCY DISTRACTION KILLER:**
If you feel the urge to procrastinate, immediately:

1. Stand up.
2. Do 10 push-ups.
3. Drink water.
4. Say: *"I am the commander of my attention."*
5. Return to work.
