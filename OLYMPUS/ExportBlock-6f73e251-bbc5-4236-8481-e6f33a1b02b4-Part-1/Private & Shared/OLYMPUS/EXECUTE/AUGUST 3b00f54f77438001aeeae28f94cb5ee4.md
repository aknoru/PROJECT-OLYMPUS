**If I feel overwhelmed...**

- "I will stop. I will breathe. I will do 25 minutes on one task."

**If I feel emotional...**

- "I will pause. I will name the emotion. I will choose the right action."

**If I feel like quitting...**

- "I will do 25 minutes. Then I can quit. But I must do 25 minutes first."

**If I miss a workout...**

- "I will do 15 minutes of movement. Any movement counts."

**If I don't hit my calorie target...**

- "I will track what I ate. Tomorrow I will do better."

**If I fail a test...**

- "I will learn from the failure. I will try again."
