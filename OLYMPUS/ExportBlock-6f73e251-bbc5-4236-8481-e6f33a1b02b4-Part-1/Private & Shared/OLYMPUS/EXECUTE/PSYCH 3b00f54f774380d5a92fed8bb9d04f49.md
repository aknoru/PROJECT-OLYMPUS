**Psychological Exercises:**

**Exercise 1: The Emotion Separation Protocol**

**When you feel a strong emotion:**

1. **Pause.** Stop all action.
2. **Name it.** "This is [emotion]."
3. **Locate it.** "I feel it in my [chest/stomach/throat]."
4. **Breathe.** 3 deep breaths.
5. **Ask:** "What is the right action here, regardless of how I feel?"
6. **Act.** Take the right action.
7. **Review.** Later, journal: "I felt [emotion]. I chose [action]. The outcome was [result]."

**Exercise 2: The Re-Scripting Practice**

- Identify one moment today when you felt triggered.
- Replay it from a third-person perspective.
- Observe yourself feeling the emotion.
- Then, rewrite the ending with your ideal response.
- Repeat three times.
