# Assessment Index

> **Generated by:** OLYMPUS Orchestrator v4 — Part V, Section 46
> **Purpose:** Every learning outcome must be measurable. This index maps every domain to its assessment instruments — quizzes, coding challenges, design reviews, and capstone evaluations.

---

## Assessment Philosophy

> "A learner has not mastered a concept until they can explain it, apply it under constraint, and debug a failure of it." — OLYMPUS Principle

Three assessment tiers are used:

| Tier | Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|------|------|-----------|---------|
| **Formative** | Quick checks, flashcards, error logs | Daily / Weekly | Identify gaps early |
| **Summative** | Chapter quizzes, coding challenges | Per module | Confirm understanding |
| **Capstone** | Design reviews, full projects | Per domain | Demonstrate mastery |
