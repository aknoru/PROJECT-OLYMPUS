## Executive Summary & System Philosophy

**PROJECT OLYMPUS** is a Markdown-native Engineering Operating System and cognitive architecture designed for total personal transformation.

The system merges two core structures:
1. **The Architecture (PROJECT OLYMPUS Scaffold):** 14 domain directories, 18 execution systems, automated validation scripts, MkDocs interactive web app, CI/CD pipeline, and formal markdown specifications.
2. **The Content (OLYMPUS Operational Brain):** Real goals (31), real targets (27), day-by-day battle plans, clinical psychological profile, identity declarations, QSIF research project, and daily/weekly/monthly tracking dashboards.

### Core Operating Principles
- **Architecture Comes First:** System integrity, file paths, and mathematical contracts are strict.
- **Consistency Beats Intensity:** A 70% day executed every day beats a 100% day followed by a 3-day collapse.
- **Proof Over Promise:** Every completed task must leave tangible evidence (code, problem solutions, commits, research notes).
- **Health is the Foundation:** Sleep (9 hrs), calories (2,500+), and physical exercise are non-negotiable prerequisites for cognitive output.
- **Private Data Remains Vaulted:** Clinical psychological profiles and personal declarations stay in `private/` and are never committed to public repositories.

## The Daily Operating Protocol (The Daily Loop)

Every day follows a structured, time-blocked rhythm designed to maximize cognitive throughput while keeping anxiety low.

```
07:00 ─── Morning Awakening & Declaration Protocol
08:00 ─── Academic Block 1: GATE / ECE Deep Work (2 hrs)
10:15 ─── Academic Block 2: DSA / CP Practice (2 hrs)
12:30 ─── Academic Block 3: AI / ML Project & Coding (1.5 hrs)
14:00 ─── Lunch & Rest Block
15:00 ─── Income Block: Freelance & AI Agency Outreach (1.5 hrs)
16:30 ─── Academic Block 4: Full-Stack / DevOps (1.5 hrs)
18:30 ─── Revision & Error Log Block (1 hr)
19:30 ─── Exercise (30 min) & Dinner
21:00 ─── Therapy Block: IFS, Somatic & Journaling (45 min)
22:00 ─── Sleep (9 Hours Non-Negotiable)
```

### Step-by-Step Daily Execution Checklist

#### 1. Morning Awakening (07:00 – 08:00)
1. **Wake immediately at 07:00.** Do not hit snooze.
2. Read the **[Why Statement](file:///c:/Users/rouna/OneDrive/Documents/PROJECT-OLYMPUS/private/identity/IDENTITY-MANIFESTO.md)** aloud.
3. Perform the **Mirror Protocol (5 min):** Stand in front of the mirror, make eye contact, recite the weekly declaration.
4. Perform **Pre-Meditation of Adversity (5 min):** Visualize potential obstacles today (e.g. difficult DSA problem, fatigue) and mentally rehearse your calm, adult response.
5. Splash cold water on face.
6. 15 minutes of light physical movement (stretching/jumping jacks).
7. Eat a high-protein breakfast and log calories in **[DAILY-DASHBOARD.md](file:///c:/Users/rouna/OneDrive/Documents/PROJECT-OLYMPUS/dashboards/DAILY-DASHBOARD.md)**.

#### 2. Academic Block 1: GATE / ECE (08:00 – 10:00)
- Focus: Linear Algebra, Calculus, Differential Equations, Networks, Signals, Devices, Analog, Digital, Controls, Communications.
- Solve 10 practice problems per session.
- Record any doubts or errors in your Error Log.

#### 3. Academic Block 2: DSA / CP (10:15 – 12:15)
- Solve 2 LeetCode problems (Medium target) or HDLBits problems.
- Write down the solution pattern (Two Pointers, Sliding Window, DP, Trees, Graph BFS/DFS).
- Maintain code commits to your DSA GitHub repository.

#### 4. Academic Block 3: AI / ML Project (12:30 – 14:00)
- Focus: PyTorch neural networks from scratch, CNNs, HuggingFace transformers, RAG pipelines, or LangChain agents.
- Work on active project repository (**rvs-accelerator** or **QSIF**).

#### 5. Income Block (15:00 – 16:30)
- Focus: AI Automation Agency client outreach, Upwork/Fiverr proposals, client deliverables.
- Target: Send 5 proposals on Mon/Wed/Fri. Target ₹5,000 in Month 1, scaling to ₹2,00,000/month long-term.

#### 6. Revision & Error Log (18:30 – 19:30)
- Review concepts from yesterday using Anki spaced repetition.
- Re-solve problems missed during previous practice sessions.

#### 7. Exercise & Nutrition (19:30 – 21:00)
- 30 minutes of progressive resistance training or cardio.
- Eat dinner. Log total daily calories (Target: 2,500 – 3,000 kcal for weight gain from 50 kg to 65–70 kg).

#### 8. Evening Therapy Block (21:00 – 21:45)
- **Observer Practice (10 min):** Sit quietly, label thoughts without judgment.
- **IFS Dialogue (15 min):** Check in with internal parts (Terrified Child, Tyrant, Groveler) and lead from the Wise Adult.
- **Somatic Release (10 min):** Progressive muscle relaxation or deep diaphragmatic breathing.
- **Gratitude Audit (5 min):** Log 3 things done well today + 1 lesson learned.
- **Journal Entry (15 min):** Reflect on emotional stability and execution.

#### 9. Wind-Down & Sleep (21:45 – 22:00)
- Screens OFF by 21:45.
- In bed by 22:00. Sleep target: 9 hours.

## The Weekly Review Protocol (Sunday Cadence)

Every Sunday between 09:00 and 12:00, execute the Weekly Review Protocol in **[WEEKLY-DASHBOARD.md](file:///c:/Users/rouna/OneDrive/Documents/PROJECT-OLYMPUS/dashboards/WEEKLY-DASHBOARD.md)**.

### The 5 Essential Questions
1. **What did I build this week?** (Code shipped, topics mastered, modules written)
2. **What habit did I maintain consistently?** (Sleep, gym, DSA, therapy)
3. **What broke down? Why?** (Identify root causes without self-punishment)
4. **What do I need to do differently next week?** (Specific tactical adjustments)
5. **Am I still on track for Day 184?** (Review progress against Phase targets)

### Weekly Audit Sequence
1. **Audit GATE & ECE Progress:** Verify practice problem counts (Target: 70+/week).
2. **Audit DSA & CP Progress:** Verify LeetCode count (Target: 10–14/week) and Codeforces rating.
3. **Audit Financials:** Check proposal count (Target: 15/week) and revenue earned.
4. **Audit Physical Metrics:** Log weekly body weight (Target: +0.5 kg/week) and average sleep hours.
5. **Execute Forgiveness Protocol:** Write a brief letter letting go of past mistakes or weekly shortcomings, read it, and release it.
6. **Set Next Week's 3 MITs (Most Important Things):** Define the 3 non-negotiable outcomes for the upcoming week.

## Operating the Interactive Command Center Web App

The repository includes a modern, high-performance local web application built with HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript, served locally via MkDocs.

```bash
# Launching the Web Application locally:
cd c:\Users\rouna\OneDrive\Documents\PROJECT-OLYMPUS
python -m pip install -r requirements.txt
mkdocs serve
```

Once running, open `http://127.0.0.1:8000` in your browser.

### Key Web App Features

1. **Header & Live Time Tracker:** Displays live date, system status, active day counter, and countdown to Jan 31, 2027.
2. **Operating Mode Switcher:**
   - **Normal Mode:** Displays full daily schedule and default target cards.
   - **Minimum Viable Day (MVD):** Activates emergency low-energy protocol (Sleep, 25-min GATE, 15-min DSA, therapy) when feeling sick or severely burnt out.
   - **Recovery Mode:** Displays emotional trauma stabilization protocol and pauses expansion commitments.
3. **Interactive Task Engine:** Add, complete, and remove daily outcomes. Progress automatically updates the progress rings and streak counters.
4. **Deep-Work Focus Pomodoro:** Built-in 25-min, 50-min, or 90-min timer with start/pause/reset controls.
5. **Proof-of-Work Evidence Logger:** Log completed engineering artifacts (Code, Problem sets, Design notes, Research). Data is stored safely in browser `localStorage`.
6. **Data Control & Export:** Import or export your progress as a JSON file to transfer between devices or backup your history.

## Troubleshooting & Emergency Recovery

### What to do when feeling overwhelmed or experiencing an emotional flashback:
1. **Activate Minimum Viable Day (MVD):** Open the Command Center web app and click **"Minimum viable day"**.
2. **Execute Emergency Protocol:**
   - Stop all new inputs. Do not open new courses or create new plans.
   - Do 25 minutes of GATE study or 1 DSA problem.
   - Perform the **Emotion Separation Protocol:** Name the feeling, locate it physically in your body, take 3 deep breaths, and ask: *"What is the right action here, regardless of how I feel?"*
3. **Consult the Recovery Route:** Read **[Resilience System](file:///c:/Users/rouna/OneDrive/Documents/PROJECT-OLYMPUS/01-operation-renaissance/11-resilience-system/README.md)**.
4. **Remember the Golden Rule:** *Consistency beats intensity. Execution beats motivation.*
