## Sleep Protocol

<div class="oa-card">
<p class="oa-label">SLEEP ENGINEERING</p>
<p class="oa-title" style="margin:.3rem 0 .5rem">Non-Negotiable Sleep Rules</p>

- **Lights out: 22:00** (non-negotiable)
- **Wake time: 07:00** (non-negotiable)
- **9 hours target** — cognitive output depends on this
- No screens after 21:45
- Blackout curtains + cold room (18–20°C optimal)
- No caffeine after 14:00

> [!NOTE]
> Sleep deprivation destroys executive function, emotional regulation, and learning consolidation. 9 hours is the mission-critical minimum, not a luxury.
