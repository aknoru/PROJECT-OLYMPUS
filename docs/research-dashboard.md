## QSIF — Project Overview

<div class="oa-card">
<p class="oa-label">BTP-II · CORE RESEARCH</p>
<p class="oa-title" style="margin:.3rem 0 .5rem">Quantum-Inspired Spatial Intelligence Fabric</p>
<p style="font-size:.85rem;color:var(--oa-text-2);margin-bottom:.75rem">A novel hardware architecture for spatial reasoning AI inspired by quantum superposition principles. Implements configurable spatial processing elements optimized for graph neural networks, 3D scene understanding, and spatial language models.</p>

**Key Innovation:** Quantum-inspired superposition of spatial representations in classical hardware, enabling exponential speedup for certain spatial reasoning tasks without requiring actual quantum hardware.

**Target Applications:**
- Autonomous vehicle scene understanding
- Robotic spatial planning
- Geospatial AI analytics
- 3D medical imaging

</div>
